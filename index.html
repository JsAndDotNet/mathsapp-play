How it works

Play

Tap the left or right half of the screen (or use the arrow keys). Sums get harder in four stages: adding, times tables, missing numbers, then BODMAS.

Keep it

On a phone, use your browser's “Add to Home Screen” and it plays like an app, even offline.

Private

No ads, no sign-up, nothing to buy. Your best scores stay on your own device.

Unlock

A new character joins every five goes. Bored of easy mode? Switch on Smart Mouth / Smarty Mode.
